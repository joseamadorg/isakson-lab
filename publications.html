Get Brant's Book!

The dinosaur era remains a physiological mystery, long limited to inferences from fossilized bones. "Balancing a Sauropod: The Physiology of a Dinosaur" breaks new ground by merging classical and extreme physiology with modern paleontology to explain how these giants truly lived.

Shifting focus from the skeleton to internal organ systems, this work tackles a fascinating question: How did sauropods thrive in the hipoxic (low-oxygen) Jurassic atmosphere? The book dissects how these massive animals adapted at a functional level to dominate their environment.

Written for both scientists and curious readers, it provides a rigorous look at the internal biology required to sustain life on a prehistoric scale. By exploring these extreme physiologies, the text offers the first comprehensive look at the dinosaur as a functioning, living animal.
